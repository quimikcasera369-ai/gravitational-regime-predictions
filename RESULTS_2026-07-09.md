# Post-registration Results — Regime Transition Predictions

**Date of analysis: 2026-07-09** (posterior to the pre-registration of 2026-02-19).
**Data:** SPARC (Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert 2016), rotation-curve mass models.
**Pre-registered scale:** `g_crit = 1.0×10⁻¹¹ m/s²` (registered 2026-03-02).

## Protocol executed (verbatim from the pre-registration)

1. Plateau detection: longest consecutive window with velocity variation < 5%
   (NOT the last 3 points); `V_flat` = mean over the window, `R_flat` = radius of
   its last point; a trailing point below the plateau is treated as an HI-edge
   artefact and excluded.
2. `g_int = V_flat² / R_flat`.
3. Classification: `g_int < g_crit` → COLLECTIVE (only these get a velocity verdict).
4. Predictions (COLLECTIVE only): `R_T = √(GM/g_crit)`, `V_pred = (GM·g_crit)^{1/4}`.
5. `error% = |V_pred − V_flat|/V_flat × 100`;  `ratio = R_T/R_flat`.
6. Verdict: `error ≤ 20% AND ratio ∈ [0.5,2.0]` → CONFIRMS; `error > 20%` → FALSIFIES.

## Selection-bias diagnostic (declared)

The classifier `g_int < g_crit` preferentially selects the low-acceleration tail:

| Group | n | median `a_impl = V⁴/GM` |
|---|---|---|
| COLLECTIVE (g_int < g_crit) | 6 | 3.4·g_crit = **0.28·a₀** |
| Rest of SPARC | 147 | 16·g_crit = **1.33·a₀** |

Because the classification threshold and the tested velocity scale are the same
quantity, the "COLLECTIVE" subsample is biased toward `g_crit`. The unbiased sample
(all galaxies) has `a_impl ≈ a₀`. The systematic under-prediction in step 6 is the
signature of this: the velocity zero-point is `a₀`, not `g_crit`.
